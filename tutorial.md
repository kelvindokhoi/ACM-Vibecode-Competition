# How to Play: Mascot Mayhem, The Rivalry Run

Help the Scot save Maryville College from the fires and toxic sludge left by
the rival Sewanee tiger. Clear each level before the timer runs out, and don't
let your health hit zero.

## Controls

| Action | Keyboard |
|---|---|
| Move left / right | `A` / `D` or `←` / `→` |
| Jump | `Space`, `W`, or `↑` |
| Spray the extinguisher | `J` (one puff per press) |
| Throw a vial | `K` |
| Restart the level | `R` |
| Continue past a menu or level card | `Enter` or `Space` |

## Your Tools

Each spray or throw uses one charge. Keep an eye on your ammo in the HUD.

| Tool | How it works | Works on |
|---|---|---|
| **Fire extinguisher** (`J`) | A short burst of foam right in front of you. Get close. | Fires and the tiger |
| **Neutralizer vial** (`K`) | Thrown in an arc. Each vial is used up on the first thing it hits. | Toxic puddles and zombies |

Foam does nothing to toxic waste, and vials do nothing to fire.

## Pickups

Walk into a pickup to collect it.

| Pickup | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Extinguisher** | +3 extinguisher charges |
| **Vial** | +3 vials |
| **Energy drink** | Speed boost for 6 seconds. The HUD shows when it's active. |

## Hazards

- **Fire:** hurts on touch. Walk up close and spray it.
- **Toxic waste:** green puddles that hurt to touch. One vial neutralizes a
  puddle.
- **Zombies (Level 3):** hurt on contact. Hit one with a vial to take it out,
  or jump over it.
- **The tiger (Level 4):** pounces and hurts on contact. Spray it, then back
  off.

After taking a hit you flash and can't be hurt again for a moment. Use that
moment to get clear.

## Level Goals

1. **Campus Dash:** run past Sutton, the MCS3 makerspace, and Anderson Hall
   and put out **80% of the fires**.
2. **Toxic Spill:** toxic waste is oozing from the chem lab. Neutralize **80%
   of the spills**, and douse any stray flames.
3. **The Gauntlet:** fire, sludge, and zombies at once. Clear **80% of the
   fires and spills** to reach the tiger.
4. **Rivalry Showdown:** hit the tiger with your extinguisher **10 times**
   before the 2-minute timer runs out. Grab refills from the ledges.

## Win and Lose

- **Win** a level by reaching its goal. Beat the tiger to save campus.
- **Lose** if your health reaches zero or the timer runs out. Press `R` to
  retry the level.

## Tips

- The extinguisher has short range. Step in, spray, step back.
- Vials arc downward, so throw them from a little distance or from above.
- Don't spam the buttons. Every press costs a charge, and there's a short
  cooldown between shots.
- Save the energy drink for long stretches or for escaping zombies.
- Against the tiger, jump its pounce, then turn and spray.
