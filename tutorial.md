# How to Play: Mascot Mayhem, The Rivalry Run

Help the Scot save Maryville College from the fires and toxic sludge left by
the rival tiger. Clear each level before the timer runs out, and don't let
your health hit zero.

## Controls

| Action | Keyboard |
|---|---|
| Move left / right | `A` / `D` or `←` / `→` |
| Jump | `W`, `↑`, or `Space` |
| Spray the current tool | `J` or `F` (hold) |
| Switch tool (extinguisher / vials) | `K` or `Q` |
| Pause | `P` or `Esc` |
| Restart the level | `R` |

## The Screen

- **Health bar** (top left): you lose health by touching fire, toxic waste,
  zombies, or the tiger. At zero, you lose.
- **Timer** (top center): clear the level before it hits zero.
- **Clearance meter:** the percentage of fires or toxic waste you've cleared.
  Reach the target to finish the level.
- **Tool meter:** how much spray is left in your current tool.
- **Boss health bar** (Level 4 only): the tiger's remaining health.

## Tools and Power-Ups

| Pickup | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Fire extinguisher** (garnet canister) | Sprays foam that puts out fires. Collect more to refill it. |
| **Neutralizer vial** (orange liquid) | Sprays neutralizer that cleans toxic waste. Fire foam won't work on sludge. Collect more vials to refill. |
| **Scot Fuel** (orange can) | Speed boost for a few seconds. You'll glow orange and leave a trail. |

Pickups float and glow above a ring. Walk into one to collect it.

## Hazards

- **Fire:** hurts on touch. Hold spray on it until it shrinks and goes out.
  Bigger fires take longer.
- **Toxic waste:** glowing green puddles that hurt to stand in. Spray them
  with vials until they turn gray.
- **Toxic zombies (Level 3):** they walk toward you and hurt on contact.
  Jump over them or outrun them; you can't defeat them.
- **The Purple Tiger (Level 4):** never touch it. Spray it with the
  extinguisher from a distance. It flashes white when hit.

## Level Goals

1. **Campus Dash:** put out at least **80% of the fires** around Sutton, the
   makerspace, and the chem lab.
2. **Toxic Spill:** switch to vials and **neutralize the toxic waste** in the
   chem lab.
3. **The Gauntlet:** clear **both fire and toxic waste** while dodging
   zombies. Grab the Scot Fuel to move faster.
4. **Rivalry Showdown:** spray the tiger until its **health bar is empty**.
   Watch it crouch before a pounce, then jump or run out of the way.

## Win and Lose

- **Win** each level by reaching its clearance goal, or by beating the tiger
  in Level 4.
- **Lose** if your health reaches zero or the timer runs out. Press `R` to
  retry the level.

## Tips

- Spray from a distance. Your stream reaches farther than the flames.
- Check your tool meter before running into a group of hazards.
- Save Scot Fuel for when the zombies close in.
- Switching tools is instant, so swap as you approach each hazard.
