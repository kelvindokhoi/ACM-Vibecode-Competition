# Storyline: Mascot Mayhem, The Rivalry Run

## Premise

It's the night before the big rivalry game, and Maryville College is on fire.

The Sewanee tiger snuck onto campus to sabotage the Scots before kickoff. It
started fires in Sutton Science Center, tipped over the chem lab's waste
drums, and slipped out into the dark. Our hero, the Scot, is the last one on
campus. The Scot grabs a fire extinguisher, pulls on the tam, and runs in.

**Goal:** save the campus, follow the trail of destruction, and chase the
tiger off for good.

## The Hero

**The Scot**: Maryville's kilted mascot in garnet and orange. Not the
fastest or the strongest, but never quits.

- Carries a **fire extinguisher** that puts out fires and refills when the
  Scot collects more extinguishers.
- Picks up **neutralizer vials** to clean up toxic waste.
- Drinks a **Scot Fuel** energy drink for a burst of speed, shown by a glowing
  orange aura and a speed trail.

## The Rival

**The Purple Tiger**: Sewanee's mascot, gone rogue. Purple, white, and gold.
It's fast, proud, and furious that the Scots keep winning. Touching it
hurts, so the Scot has to keep their distance and fight it with the
extinguisher.

## Level 1: Campus Dash

*"Sutton is burning!"*

The Scot arrives at Sutton Science Center to find flames in the windows. The
path runs past Anderson Hall Tower and the Covenant Stone, through the MCS3
makerspace (3D printers still running) and into the chem lab.

- **Beginning:** the campus lawn outside Sutton, with the first small fires
  and a spare extinguisher.
- **Middle:** the makerspace, where fires spread between the printers and
  workbenches.
- **Finish:** the chem lab doors.
- **Win:** put out at least 80% of the fires before time runs out.
- **Clue:** muddy paw prints lead deeper into the lab.

## Level 2: Toxic Spill

*"Water won't work on this."*

The tiger knocked over the chem lab's waste drums. Glowing green sludge
spreads across the floor, and the extinguisher can't touch it. The Scot finds
a rack of neutralizer vials: the only way to clean the waste.

- **Beginning:** spilled drums at the lab entrance.
- **Middle:** the fume hoods and benches, with leaking containers dripping
  from above.
- **Finish:** the back exit, where a trail of claw marks leads outside.
- **Win:** neutralize enough toxic waste before time runs out.

## Level 3: The Gauntlet

*"Something is crawling out of the sludge..."*

Fire and toxic waste have mixed, and the waste is spawning toxic zombies that
shamble toward the Scot. The Scot has to swap between extinguisher and vials
while dodging zombies on the run back across campus. Halfway through, the Scot
finds a can of Scot Fuel.

- **Beginning:** the scorched lab hallway.
- **Middle:** the campus quad, now full of fire, sludge, and zombies.
- **Finish:** the athletic field gate, wide open and pulsing with purple light.
- **Win:** clear enough of both hazards while avoiding the zombies.
- **New twist:** the zombies, plus the speed power-up to outrun them.

## Level 4: Rivalry Showdown

*"So it was YOU."*

On the field, the Purple Tiger stands under its banners, roaring. It pounces,
swipes, and sets new fires, and touching it means damage. The Scot sprays it
again and again with the extinguisher until it's soaked, stunned, and beaten.

- **Beginning:** the tiger enters and roars to start the fight.
- **Middle:** the tiger's attacks get faster as its health drops, and it
  leaves fire and toxic puddles behind.
- **Finish:** the soaked tiger gives up and runs off campus.
- **Win:** drain the tiger's health bar to zero.

## Ending

The fires are out, the sludge is clean, and the sun comes up over Anderson
Hall. The scoreboard lights up garnet and orange.

**Victory screen:** "Campus saved! The Scots rule the rivalry."

## Failure

At any point the Scot can lose by:

- **Taking too much damage** from fire, toxic waste, zombies, or the tiger.
  "The Scot is down! The tiger wins this round."
- **Running out of time.** "Too late! The campus burned. Try again?"

The player can retry from the start of the current level.
